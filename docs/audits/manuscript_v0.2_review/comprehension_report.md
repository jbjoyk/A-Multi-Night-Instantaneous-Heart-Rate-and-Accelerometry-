## Paraphrase of Feature Specification

This manuscript presents a clinical and algorithmic evaluation of 4-class sleep staging (Wake, Light, Deep, REM) from low-rate (~0.2 Hz) Apple Watch instantaneous heart rate (IHR) and tri-axial accelerometry across two public healthy-adult cohorts: BIDSleep (`N=47`, 246 multi-night home recordings paired with technologist-reviewed Dreem 2 dry-sensor EEG) and PhysioNet SleepAccel (`N=31` total; `n=25` quality-clean single-night laboratory polysomnography [PSG] recordings). The study evaluates three core clinical-translational questions: (1) whether a lightweight 163,940-parameter convolutional bidirectional recurrent architecture (Model A, compressed via Dynamic INT8 quantisation to `309.54 KiB`) can match the 33.34-fold larger published SLAMSS-IFS server architecture (`5,465,768` parameters) under strictly subject-disjoint 5-fold cross-validation and external zero-shot PSG transfer; (2) how many labeled personal calibration nights (`k=1`–`4`) are needed for within-subject supervised fine-tuning (`FT-2` at `k=2` across `N=46`, and `k=1`–`4` scaling on a common 33-subject cohort `E_4`) to outperform a frozen population backbone (`ZS`) as well as donor-subject (`FT-other-2`), from-scratch (`PS-2`), and unsupervised entropy-minimization (`UA-2`) controls; and (3) whether pre-adaptation stage-coverage abstention gating (`GATE_tau`, reverting to `ZS` when any sleep stage represents `<1%` of calibration epochs) prevents severe negative transfer while preserving clinical sleep-summary agreement (Total Sleep Time [TST], Sleep Onset Latency [SOL], Wake After Sleep Onset [WASO], Sleep Efficiency [SE], Deep%, and REM%).

## Paraphrase of Detailed Implementation Plan

The study operationalizes its design across two sequential validation stages using full-night 10-hour sequences (`L=1200` 30-second epochs) constructed from 1 Hz resampled IHR, accelerometry magnitude/jerk/energy features, per-night median/IQR normalization, and circadian-neutralized metadata. In the internal BIDSleep cohort (`N=47`, 246 nights after excluding 7 nights with `<60%` sensor coverage), subject-disjoint 5-fold cross-validation across 3 random seeds (`42, 123, 2024`) benchmarks frozen FP32 and Dynamic INT8 Model A against the official SLAMSS-IFS repository architecture trained without teacher-forcing leakage. Within-subject adaptation is tested on `N=46` participants (`>=3` usable nights; 152 evaluation nights) by fine-tuning on each subject's first `k=2` chronological nights (`FT-2`) using Adam (`B=2`, gradient clip `1.0`) with nested inner-fold hyperparameter selection, compared against `ZS`, `FT-other-2`, `PS-2`, and remediated InfoMax unsupervised adaptation (`UA-2`), while calibration-depth scaling (`k=1`–`4`) is evaluated on the common cohort `N=33` (`E_4`, 64 nights) and full available cohorts (`E_k`, `N=46/46/42/33`), followed by post-hoc exploratory Spearman heterogeneity checks and `KL` / `GATE_tau` safety guardrails. Finally, all 15 frozen BIDSleep checkpoints (5 folds $\times$ 3 seeds) for both Model A and SLAMSS-IFS are evaluated strictly zero-shot on PhysioNet SleepAccel single-night in-lab PSG (`n=25` QC-clean primary after excluding 5 recordings with `<600` epochs and 1 with severe motion dropout; `N=31` sensitivity) under both single-model mean (`mean_single_model`) and probability-ensemble (`prob_ensemble`) modes, quantifying epoch-level Macro-F1 via 10,000-resample participant-level percentile bootstrap 95% CIs and Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, alongside subject-level Bland–Altman clinical agreement (`SE(LoA)`, proportional bias regression, and Shapiro–Wilk normality fallbacks).
