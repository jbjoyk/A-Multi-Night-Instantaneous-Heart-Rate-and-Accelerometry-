## 2. Research Objective

To investigate whether anchoring personal fine-tuning to initial zero-shot representations via Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence regularization, or abstaining from adaptation via stage-coverage gating ($\tau$-gating), mitigates catastrophic personal degradation (worst-case subject loss $\le -5.0\text{ pp}$) without sacrificing the positive population-level adaptation gain.

---

## 3. Experimental Arms & Architecture

### Model Architecture
All adaptation arms utilize the frozen project baseline: **Model A** (`ConventionalCNNBiGRU`, exactly 163,940 parameters; $L=1200$ whole-night context, sliding-window inference with probability stitching).

### Experimental Arms (4 Total)
1. **Arm 1: Zero-Shot Baseline ($ZS$)**  
   Pre-trained cohort model evaluated without subject-specific adaptation. Predictions reused bitwise from locked EXP-019 Stage C.
2. **Arm 2: Standard 2-Night Supervised Fine-Tuning ($FT\text{-}2$)**  
   Standard cross-entropy fine-tuning on the subject's first 2 labeled nights. Predictions reused bitwise from locked EXP-019 Stage C.
3. **Arm 3: KL-Regularized Fine-Tuning ($FT\text{-}2\text{-KL}$)**  
   Fine-tuning on the first 2 labeled nights with an auxiliary penalty penalizing divergence from the unadapted model's output distribution.
4. **Arm 4: Stage-Coverage Gated Adaptation ($\text{GATE}_\tau$)**  
   A decision-rule meta-arm: evaluate calibration-night class coverage. If all 4 sleep stages meet the coverage threshold $\tau$, deploy $FT\text{-}2$; otherwise, abstain and deploy $ZS$.

## 4. Mathematical Formulations & Objective Functions

### 4.1 KL Regularization Loss ($FT\text{-}2\text{-KL}$)
During calibration adaptation, the objective function combines standard task Cross-Entropy with a Kullback-Leibler divergence penalty against frozen pre-adaptation logits:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}(\theta; \mathcal{D}_{\text{cal}}) = \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}(y, \hat{y}_\theta) + \lambda_{\text{KL}} \cdot D_{\text{KL}}\left( \sigma(z_{\text{ZS}} / T) \parallel \sigma(z_\theta / T) \right)$$
where:
- $\hat{y}_\theta = \sigma(z_\theta)$ are predicted softmax probabilities from the adapting model parameterized by $\theta$.
- $z_{\text{ZS}}$ are frozen logits generated by the unadapted Zero-Shot model ($T = 1.0$).
- $\lambda_{\text{KL}} \in \{0.1, 1.0, 10.0\}$ is a hyperparameter governing anchor strength.

### 4.2 Gating Decision Rule ($\text{GATE}_\tau$)
For subject $i$, let $P_{C, i}(y=c)$ denote the empirical class frequency of stage $c \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ across their 2 calibration nights. The deployed prediction vector $\hat{Y}_i$ on evaluation nights is governed by:
$$\hat{Y}_i = \begin{cases} \hat{Y}_{i, FT\text{-}2} & \text{if } \min_{c \in \{0,1,2,3\}} P_{C, i}(y=c) \ge \tau \\ \hat{Y}_{i, ZS} & \text{if } \min_{c \in \{0,1,2,3\}} P_{C, i}(y=c) < \tau \end{cases}$$

## 5. Partition Separation & Hyperparameter Policy

- **Nested Cross-Validation:** Identical 5-fold outer cross-validation across all 47 subjects (`review_package/fold_manifests.csv`).
- **Inner-Validation Hyperparameter Selection ($\lambda_{\text{KL}}$):**  
  For each outer fold $k \in \{1, \dots, 5\}$, $\lambda_{\text{KL}} \in \{0.1, 1.0, 10.0\}$ is selected strictly using that fold's **5 inner-validation subjects** based on mean inner-validation Macro-F1 improvement. The outer test subjects are never evaluated during selection.
- **Gating Threshold Sensitivity ($\tau$):**  
  Because 5 inner-validation subjects per fold provide insufficient sample size to reliably tune continuous percentage thresholds, **$\tau$ is NOT tuned**. Instead, the protocol specifies a prospective sensitivity sweep over a fixed grid:
  $$\tau \in \{1.0\%, 2.0\%, 3.0\%, 5.0\%\} \quad \text{[`TO VERIFY` for final threshold selection]}$$
  Results must be reported across all four values as a transparent sensitivity curve.

## 6. Endpoints, Metrics, and Evaluation Rules

All endpoints are strictly **descriptive** and evaluated on outer evaluation nights across the 46 eligible subjects:
1. **Mean Paired Improvement:** $\bar{\Delta} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{46} (\text{Macro-F1}_{\text{Arm}} - \text{Macro-F1}_{\text{ZS}})$.
2. **Worst-Case Degradation (Minimum $\Delta$):** $\min_{i} \Delta_i$ (specifically evaluating whether catastrophic drop is bounded above $-5.0\text{ pp}$).
3. **10th Percentile Subject $\Delta$:** Robust lower-tail behavior under adaptation.
4. **Catastrophic Failure Count:** Number of subjects experiencing severe degradation: $N_{\text{harm}} = \sum_{i=1}^{46} \mathbb{I}(\Delta_i \le -5.0\text{ pp})$.
5. **Mean Gain Among Adapted Subjects:** Average delta computed exclusively over subjects where adaptation was triggered ($\text{Gate} = 1$).
6. **Uncertainty Reporting:** 95% percentile bootstrap confidence intervals computed over 10,000 subject-level resamples.
