- **Date:** 2026-11-17
- **Search Scope:** Full-landscape scale-up across wearable PPG/IHR/accel sleep staging, multi-night/personalized sleep staging, DA/DG, TTA collapse/label shift, negative transfer, selective prediction/abstention/gating, uncertainty/calibration, continual learning, external/clinical Bland-Altman validation, and data leakage.

- **Changed Conclusions:** None (`LOCKED` research direction and EXP-019 verdicts remain immutable).
- **Notable Phase 3 Insights:**
  1. **Quality-Gated Adaptation Precedent:** `P075` ([BeatRhythm-TTA 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23347)) introduces Signal Quality Index (SQI)-gated adaptation on cardiac ECG to prevent artifact-driven drift—directly parallel to pre-adaptation gating on wearable IHR.
  2. **Uncertainty-Guided Sleep Staging:** `P037` ([SleepTransformer 2022](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.11043v3)) and `P090` ([PubMed 39112022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39112022/)) show uncertainty quantification improves wearable EEG 5-stage accuracy (`63.9% -> 71.2%`), while `P091` ([van Gorp et al. 2022](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/361180445_Certainty_about_uncertainty_in_sleep_staging_a_theoretical_framework)) separates aleatoric (`82.6%` inter-scorer ceiling) from epistemic uncertainty.
  3. **Apnea & Age Degradation:** `P023` ([Radha et al. 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6775145/)) and `P030` ([medRxiv 2026](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.09.26350266v3.full-text)) confirm HRV/wearable staging degrades with age ($\ge 50$) and OSA severity.
  4. **Leakage Quantification:** `P101` ([Brookshire et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.27033v1)), `P102` ([MDPI 2026](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-4990/8/11/379)), and `P103` ([PMC13680413](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13680413/)) quantify 24–52 percentage-point inflation from leaky segment-wise splits.
  5. **Commercial Clinical Margins:** `P039` ([Schyvens et al. 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12038347/)) and `P040` ([Robbins et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11511193/)) show commercial wearables underestimate WASO by 12–48 min and Apple Watch underestimates Deep sleep by ~43 min.
